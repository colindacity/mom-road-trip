## Old Faithful (Wed afternoon)
- Eruptions every ~90min, predicted times posted at Old Faithful Visitor Education Center
- Watch from boardwalk benches — flat, accessible, perfect for Mom
- After eruption, walk Upper Geyser Basin loop counter-clockwise (~1mi flat boardwalk) past Castle, Grand, Beehive — quieter than main viewing area
- Step inside **Old Faithful Inn lobby** (1904 log architecture — worth 10min even if not eating)

## Grand Prismatic — DO Fairy Falls Overlook
**Skip the Midway boardwalk crowd.** The Fairy Falls trailhead overlook is the iconic shot AND senior-friendly:
- 1.2mi RT, 105ft elevation gain over 0.6mi (one short uphill)
- Park at Fairy Falls Trailhead (1mi south of Midway Geyser Basin)
- "Very well-suited for seniors… moderately graded slope, only 0.2mi uphill to viewing platform" — NPS

If Mom is tired, **Midway Geyser Basin boardwalk** (0.5mi flat loop right at the spring) is the backup.

## Lamar Valley — Manage Expectations
- Wolves and grizzlies are dawn/dusk sightings
- **Midday late May = bison herds + pronghorn (almost guaranteed) + maybe a bear at distance**
- Bring binoculars
- Best pullouts: Slough Creek and Little America
- Don't push past Slough Creek — adds 30+ min each way for diminishing returns

## Wildlife Safety
- 100yd from bears/wolves, 25yd from bison/elk/everything else
- **Bison cause more injuries than bears** — they look slow, they aren't
- Bison jams normal. Stay in car, be patient
- Bear spray optional for itinerary (not bushwhacking off-trail)

## West Yellowstone Dining (walking distance from Crosswinds, 201 Firehole Ave, all under 0.3mi)
- **Madison Crossing Lounge** — historic schoolhouse, bison tenderloin, best dinner
- **Wild West Pizzeria & Saloon** (14 Madison Ave) — casual, fast, good after long day
- **Three Bear Restaurant** — classic, reliable
- **Cafe Madriz** — Spanish tapas, smaller portions good for Mom

## Weather & Pack
- Late May: 50-60°F days, 30-40°F nights
- **Snow possible at Dunraven Pass (8,800ft)**
- Pack: warm layers, gloves, beanie, waterproof shell, sunscreen, water, snacks, binoculars, America the Beautiful pass
- Top off gas at Madison Junction or Old Faithful
