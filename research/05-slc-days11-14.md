## Day 14 Saturday — order matters
**Best sequence: Antelope Island AM → NHMU PM → Ensign Peak sunset**

**9am Antelope Island** (45min drive, $10/car). Morning = fewer gnats, more bison movement. **CRITICAL: May is peak no-see-um/biting gnat season — bring fine-mesh head nets** (insect repellent doesn't work). Buffalo Point breezier, fewer bugs. Buffalo Point easy walk for 360° Great Salt Lake view, drive to Fielding Garr Ranch. Out by 12:30pm

**2-5pm NHMU at U of U** ($20.95 senior, $22.95 adult — note slightly higher than the $18/$20 mentioned). 10am-5pm daily. Must-see: Past Worlds dinosaurs, Native Voices, gems & minerals, Bug World special exhibit. Rooftop terrace incredible valley view

**7pm Ensign Peak** (sunset ~8:35pm). **Trail is 0.9mi RT, not 0.8.** Steep but short — "appropriate for beginners" but knees will feel it. **Hiking poles strongly recommended for Mom.** ~20min up. Bring headlamp for descent. If knees flare, swap for **State Capitol grounds at sunset** (also panoramic, no climb)
