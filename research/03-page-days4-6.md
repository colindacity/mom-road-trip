## Upper Antelope Canyon Tour (Day 5, 10am MST)
- **Office: Antelope Slot Canyon Tours, 22 South Lake Powell Blvd, Page AZ 86040**
- Free parking at office
- **Arrive 9:30am minimum** for check-in
- **Bag policy: NO bags, fanny packs, hydration packs, backpacks**. Only: phone, camera, ONE water bottle in hand. **Leave purses in car.**
- **Banned:** tripods, monopods, selfie sticks, drones, GoPro chest mounts. Phone or handheld only
- **Camera tips:** Phone HDR mode handles contrast. For light beams, expose for the beam (tap to focus on bright area), let surrounding rock fall dark. Guides throw sand into beams
- **Total elapsed:** ~2.5hrs (15min orient + 20min truck + 60-75min in canyon + return). Done by ~12:30pm
- **Waiver:** Antelope Slot Canyon Tours sends digital via email after booking. Mom must sign electronically — if not, signs in person at office (10min, no real risk)
- **Light beams in May:** Peak season. 10am tour at leading edge of beam window (peak 11am-1:30pm); beams visible as sun climbs
- **Navajo park fee included** in tour price (Order #FMBYMK already paid all fees)
- **Tour operates on MST (Arizona time)** — NOT Navajo MDT (verified: their website says "We Operate on MST")
