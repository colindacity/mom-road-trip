## Teton Pass (WY-22 / ID-33) Late May Status
- Open year-round, **late May = clear** (avalanche season Dec-Apr)
- Steep 10% grade with switchbacks; SUV fine, no chains/RV restrictions in May
- **Drive mid-morning (after 9am)** to avoid icy overnight refreeze and rare late-spring afternoon thunderstorms
- Allow **35-45min Victor → Jackson** via the pass
