# Grand Canyon Days 2-3 — Mon-Wed May 11-13, 2026

## Drive Vegas → GC (Mon May 11)
- Route: US-93 S → Kingman → I-40 E → exit 165 (Williams) → AZ-64 N to South Entrance, ~280mi/4.5hr
- Leave Vegas ~9am PT. Cross to MST near Hoover Dam (AZ +1hr year-round, no DST)
- **Gas:** Top off in Kingman or Williams. NOT Tusayan (pricey, lines)
- **Lunch in Williams (best):**
  - Pine Country Restaurant — sit-down, fast, famous pies, mom-friendly
  - Goldie's Route 66 Diner — quick diner photo
  - **Skip Seligman** — adds 30+ min, save energy for sunset
- Rest stops: Hoover Dam overlook (5min), Kingman, Williams
- Arrive South Entrance ~3:30pm MST (lines <15min)

## Park Entry
- Use **South Entrance (Tusayan)** from Vegas
- Lines stack 30-90min at peak (10am-2pm); afternoon arrival = short
- Buy America the Beautiful $80 (covers Mom + Robin as passengers)

## Mather Point Sunset (Mon)
- **Sunset May 11 = 7:24pm MST**, twilight glow until 7:50pm
- Park Visitor Center Lots 1-4 (Lot 4 closest). **Arrive 6:00-6:15pm**
- Mather is 5min flat paved walk from Lot 1
- **Walk 5min east on Rim Trail to "Mather Point East"** for fewer crowds
- Or walk 10min west to Yavapai Point

## Hermit Road (Tue afternoon)
- Private cars CLOSED Mar-Nov, Red Route shuttle only
- First bus 8am, last 1hr after sunset, ~80min RT non-stop
- **Mom-friendly stops (don't do all 9):**
  - Powell Point (short flat walk)
  - **Hopi Point** — THE sunset spot, pit toilets, biggest views
  - Mohave Point — quieter, equally stunning, benches
  - SKIP: The Abyss, Pima
- Walk between stops on paved Rim Trail, reboard at any westbound stop
- Returning east: only stops at Hopi/Mohave/Powell/Hermits Rest

## Day 3 Rim Trail (Tue)
- **Best mom section: Yavapai Geology Museum → Mather → Visitor Center**
- 0.7mi one-way (1.4mi RT), fully paved, 8ft wide, wheelchair-grade
- Benches every 0.2mi, near-flat
- Extension if Mom strong: Yavapai → Verkamp's (1.7mi one-way, ends at Bright Angel Lodge for lunch)
- Restrooms: Visitor Center, Yavapai, Bright Angel Lodge
- Limited shade — Ponderosa pine clumps

## El Tovar Lunch — Walk-in
- Yes walk-ins for lunch but routinely turned away peak season
- **Arrive 11:15am** (opens 11:30), 30-60min wait, no guarantee
- **Backups (walk-in friendly):**
  1. **Arizona Steakhouse at Bright Angel Lodge** — lunch 11:30-3:30, walk-ins fine, canyon-view windows
  2. Bright Angel Fountain / Coffee House — fast/casual, rim-side
  3. El Tovar Lounge — no res, smaller bar menu
  4. Maswik Food Court — fastest, cheapest
- **Play:** Put name in El Tovar 11:15, walk 2min to Bright Angel as fallback

## Yavapai Geology Museum
- Free with park pass, open 8am-7pm
- Plan 30-45min
- Must-see: floor-to-ceiling north window with rock layers labeled, giant 3-D scale relief map
- Free 30-min ranger geology talks daily
- Best mid-morning when sun lights north wall

## Altitude (7,000ft)
- Real but rarely serious
- Mom risk profile (80, active hiker) is fine
- Expect: shorter breath on stairs, mild day-1 fatigue, possible mild headache
- **Tactics:** 3-4L water/day, electrolytes (LMNT or Liquid IV) in one bottle, half-portion alcohol night 1, no big climbs day 1, ibuprofen for headache

## Photo Timing
- Mather Point: Sunset (east-facing, shadow-filled canyon) and sunrise
- Hopi Point: Best sunset (west-facing peninsula)
- Yavapai Point: Mid-morning, clean north light
- Hermit Rd: 1hr before sunset

## Maswik Lodge
- Maswik Food Court (breakfast 6-10am, lunch/dinner 11am-10pm) + Pizza Pub (4-10pm beer + slices)
- Free parking Lots C/D
- Village (Blue) shuttle stops at front entrance every 15min
- 1/4mi flat walk to rim from lodge

## Pack
- Layers: fleece + windproof shell (sunset 7K can drop to 45°F)
- Water: 1.5L bottle each, refill at Visitor Center, Yavapai, Maswik
- Sun: brimmed hat, SPF 30+, sunglasses (humidity often <10%)
- Snacks: trail mix, jerky, fruit
- Trekking pole for Mom
- Headlamp for post-sunset walk back
- Cash $5-10 for tips

## Mom-Friendly Pacing — Don't Try to "Do" the Canyon
**Day 2 (arrival):** Mather Point sunset only. Dinner Maswik. Bed early.

**Day 3 full day — three viewpoints, that's it:**
1. **Morning:** Yavapai Museum + Rim Trail walk to Mather (1.4mi RT)
2. **Lunch:** El Tovar attempt → Bright Angel backup
3. **Afternoon:** Hermit Rd shuttle to Hopi Point only (or +Mohave if energy), back by 5pm
4. **Evening:** Optional 2nd sunset at Hopi (shuttle out 5:45pm) OR rest

Three viewpoints done well > eight done exhausted.
