# Mom Travel Brief — 80yo Canadian Active Woman, Vegas to Glacier

## 1. Cross-Border Meds (Canada → US)
- **Original pharmacy bottles only**, name matching passport. CBP can refuse loose pills
- **Carry-on, NOT checked.** Lost prescriptions = trip-ending
- 90-day supply max per US Customs. Bring written prescription copies + doctor letter for controlled substances
- Generic equivalents are interchangeable
- **Bring 1-week buffer** beyond trip length
- **Print medication list** (drug, dose, schedule, doctor, allergies) — wallet + phone

## 2. Travel Insurance (CRITICAL)
- **OHIP/provincial covers ~5-10% of US medical costs.** Single ER visit $3,000+ USD; hospital $10K+/day
- **Get supplemental travel medical insurance entire 22 days.** Manulife, Blue Cross, TuGo, Allianz Canada all offer 80+ plans. Expect $200-400 for trip
- **Verify pre-existing condition coverage** — most policies require 90-180 day stability period. Declare honestly or claims denied
- Check credit card coverage first (most cap at age 65 or 75)
- **Carry policy number + 24/7 claims line** in wallet, photo on phone

## 3. Knee/Joint Support
- **Trekking poles:** Black Diamond Trail Ergo Cork or Leki Legacy Lite. Collapsible. **Must go in CHECKED bag** — TSA prohibits carry-on. Reduces knee load 25% on descents
- **Knee sleeves:** Bauerfeind GenuTrain or McDavid 401 — compression + warmth, not bulky. One per knee
- **KT Tape Pro** pre-cut strips — patellar support for hiking days
- **Compression socks:** 15-20 mmHg, knee-high. Sockwell or CEP. Wear on flights + 3+ hr drives
- **Glucosamine + chondroitin:** if she takes it, start 2 weeks before. Won't help if started day-of

## 4. Altitude
- **Highest exposure:** Grand Canyon South Rim ~7,000ft, Yellowstone Lake ~7,700ft, Logan Pass ~6,600ft
- **At 80, AMS risk real.** Symptoms: headache, nausea, poor sleep, breathlessness on mild exertion
- **Pace rules:** First day at 7K+ = no hiking, just light walking. Sleep low when possible
- **Hydration:** +1L/day at altitude on top of baseline. Alcohol hits 2x harder
- **Descend if:** confusion, severe headache unresponsive to ibuprofen, vomiting, ataxia

## 5. Desert Hydration (Page, Moab, Vegas)
- **Target 3-4L/day** in desert heat for elderly (vs 2L baseline)
- **Elderly thirst signal is dulled** — drink on schedule, not thirst
- **LMNT or Nuun** electrolyte tabs — 1-2/day in desert. LMNT has more sodium (1000mg), better for heat
- **Heat exhaustion in elderly:** confusion, weak pulse, cool/clammy skin, no sweating despite heat. Get to AC immediately, cool with wet towels, sip water. 911 if confusion persists
- **1.5L insulated bottle** (Hydro Flask 40oz) + refill at every visitor center

## 6. Sun Protection
- **Daily SPF 50 mineral** (zinc-based — EltaMD UV Clear or Blue Lizard). Reapply every 2hrs
- **Wide-brim hat** (Tilley T3 or Sunday Afternoons Adventure) — not baseball cap. Ears + neck burn fast on thin skin
- **UPF 50 long-sleeve sun shirt** (Coolibar, REI Sahara) — cooler than sunscreen-only
- **Wraparound polarized sunglasses** with UV400. Cataracts + macular degeneration risk at 80

## 7. Footwear
- **Hiking shoes (NOT boots) for active 80yo:** Hoka Anacapa Low or Merrell Moab 3 Low. Cushioned, ankle freedom, lighter than boots. Boots only for ankle instability — extra weight = more knee strain
- **City walking:** Hoka Bondi or Brooks Ghost Max. Max cushion
- **Slip-ons for car/airport:** Allbirds Wool Loungers or Skechers Hands-Free. Critical for TSA + frequent car exits
- **Three pairs total.** Rotate to dry

## 8. Layering (95°F desert → 30°F mountain morning, 65°F+ swing)
- **Base:** Smartwool Classic All-Season Merino crew + bottoms. Anti-stink, warm when wet, packable
- **Mid:** Patagonia R1 fleece OR Uniqlo Ultra Light Down jacket. Light puffy more versatile
- **Outer:** Rain shell with hood (Marmot PreCip or REI Rainier). Cuts wind too — Glacier and Yellowstone get sudden squalls

## 9. ADHD-Friendly Travel
- **One-page daily card:** date, drive time, 1-2 activities, dinner reservation, sleep location. Big fonts
- **Buffer time:** add 30min between every transition. ADHD time-blindness + 80yo pace = always behind
- **Skip info dumps.** No park ranger talks longer than 20min
- **Long drives = audiobook or podcast.** Plays to skim-tendency. Chapters > one long story
- **Engagement zones:** scenic viewpoints (5-10min), short walks (under 1mi), meals
- **Zone-out zones:** 3+ hr drives, museum text panels, ranger lectures

## 10. Sleep
- **Eye mask** (Manta Sleep — adjustable cup, doesn't press eyes)
- **Loop Quiet earplugs** or Mack's silicone — Airbnbs near parks have thin walls + early hikers
- **White noise app** if no machine
- **Melatonin 1-3mg** for time zone shifts (3hr west). 30min before bedtime. NOT 10mg gummies — too much for elderly

## 11. Bathroom Strategy
- **Plan stops every 90min on driving days.** Gas stations, visitor centers, McDonald's reliable
- **In parks:** bathrooms only at visitor centers + major trailheads. **Pee before every trail**
- **AllStays or iOverlander apps** show bathroom locations off interstate
- **Pack travel TP + hand sanitizer + small wet wipes** in day bag

## 12. Mobility Aids
- **Foldable seat cane** (Drive Medical Folding Lightweight) — doubles as walking aid + sit-anywhere for waits
- Trekking poles double as walking sticks in town

## 13. Food
- **Smaller appetite normal at 80.** Don't force big restaurant meals — share plates, order appetizers as mains
- **Car snacks:** trail mix, cheese sticks, apples, RXBars, beef jerky, crackers. Avoid messy/sugary
- **Common 80yo issues:** lactose intolerance, blood sugar dips, GERD
- **Fiber matters** — travel constipation is real. Prunes, oatmeal at breakfast

## 14. Long Flights (YYZ-LAS = 4h47m, FCA-YYZ = 5h+ via MSP)
- **Compression socks BEFORE boarding.** DVT risk doubles after age 60 on flights >4hrs
- **Aisle seat** — get up every 60-90min, walk to back, calf pumps
- **1 cup water per hour airborne.** Skip alcohol + caffeine
- **Lumbar pillow** (Cabeau Evolution S3) or rolled jacket

## 15. Cell Phone
- **Easiest: Rogers/Bell/Telus US roaming day pass** ($12-15/day). Auto-bills, no setup. 22 days = ~$300
- Alt: US prepaid eSIM (Airalo USA 20GB $26 for 30 days). Requires unlock + comfort with eSIM swap
- **Recommendation: roaming day pass.** Worth the premium for zero friction
- **Download offline maps** (Google Maps + AllTrails) for park dead zones

## 16. Emergency Contacts
- **911** — works everywhere in US
- **Canadian Embassy DC:** +1-844-880-6519 (24/7 emergency line)
- **Travel insurer 24/7 claims** — saved in phone
- **Family ICE contact** + Colin's number on lock screen (iPhone Medical ID)

## 17. Easy-to-Forget Essentials
- 2 pairs reading glasses + 1 prescription pair
- Phone charger + 10,000mAh power bank (Anker)
- Aquaphor lip balm (desert destroys lips in days)
- Refresh preservative-free eye drops (dry park air)
- 1-week buffer of every prescription
- Hearing aid spare batteries (size 312 most common)
- Nail clippers in checked bag
- Small first-aid kit (Band-Aids, ibuprofen, Imodium, antihistamine, hydrocortisone)
- Kleenex, dental floss
