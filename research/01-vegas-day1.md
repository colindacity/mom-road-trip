# Day 1 — Las Vegas, Sun May 10, 2026

## LAS Airport → LINQ
- **Recommend rideshare from LAS, pick up rental car next morning.** Mom is jet-lagged 3hr from Toronto.
- Rideshare $10-20 + $4.50 LAS surcharge, ~10-15min ride
- Terminal 1 = Level 2M; Terminal 3 = Valet level
- If renting same-day: shuttle to RAC + 10-15min drive = ~45-60min vs ~20min Uber
- LINQ self-park $18/day, valet $35

## Bacchanal Logistics
- Walk from LINQ: south, across LINQ Promenade, cross Flamingo Rd at pedestrian bridge, enter Caesars from Strip. AC indoor. ~10-12min slow walk
- Inside Caesars from Strip: right at lobby → left past tables → through slots past Julius Tower → Apostrophe Bar → buffet
- Dress: Smart casual. Closed-toe for Mom (cold floors)
- Sunday 5:30pm = **dinner menu** (better — lobster claws "two ways," 3 crab varieties, prime rib, t-bone, bone marrow, Cajun seafood boil)
- **Order strategy for Mom:**
  - HIT FIRST: King crab legs, lobster claws, prime rib, lamb chops
  - SKIP: pasta, pizza, generic turkey, salads
  - SLEEPER HITS: Korean fried chicken, quesabirria tacos
  - DESSERT: macarons, gelato, mini crème brûlée
- Small plates, sit, eat, return — don't pile high
- Time: 1.5-2hrs

## Bellagio Fountains
- Sunday 8pm-midnight every 15min
- **Best seated viewing for Mom:** Beer Park at Paris Las Vegas — outdoor patio across the street, cost of one drink
- Best free standing: center sidewalk rail in front of Bellagio main entrance, arrive ~5min early
- Free benches along lake-walk path north & south of main viewing area

## Mom Tips
- Time-zone: 8pm Vegas = 11pm Toronto, plan horizontal by 9:30pm
- **Strip is 4.2 miles end-to-end** — looks short on map but resorts are massive
- Bellagio↔LINQ = ~3,900-4,800ft, 12-18min slow walk
- Free indoor restrooms: Bellagio Conservatory bathroom (nicest)
- Free ARIA Express tram connects Bellagio↔Cosmo↔ARIA
- Indoor routes when possible (Caesars↔Bellagio half-indoor)
