## Day 7 Sat — Page → Moab via Monument Valley (270mi/~6.5hr drive)
- **Sunrise/sunset Moab:** 6:09am / 8:24pm

**Route + fuel CRITICAL:**
1. Page → US-98 E → US-160 E → Kayenta (~2hr, 125mi). **Fuel up in Page** — only one gas station between
2. Kayenta — **mandatory fuel + restroom**, last reliable gas before Mexican Hat
3. Kayenta → US-163 N → Monument Valley (~25min)
4. Monument Valley → Mexican Hat → Bluff → Blanding → Monticello → Moab (~150mi, 2.5hr)

**Monument Valley stop (~2hr):**
- **View Hotel deck = FREE viewpoint** of West/East Mitten + Merrick Butte. Skip the $8/person + $15 vehicle Tribal Park fee if low energy
- Hours 7am-8pm, last vehicle 6pm
- **SKIP the 17-mile loop drive** — natural dirt with deep sand, not rental-car friendly, 2-3hrs. The View deck gives 80% of the photos for 0% of the risk
- **Forrest Gump Point** = Mile Marker 13 on US-163 NORTH of park (heading to Mexican Hat — on your route). Pullouts on left

**Lunch:** Twin Rocks Cafe in Bluff (913 E Navajo Twins Dr), 8am-2pm — Navajo tacos, blue-corn pancakes. **Closes 2pm — adjust departure.** If late, Comb Ridge Eat & Drink in Bluff or push to Moab Brewery dinner

**Suggested timing:** Depart Page 12:30pm after work → Kayenta 2:30pm gas → View Hotel deck 3:15pm → Forrest Gump 4:30pm → Moab Brewery 7:30pm
