## 6. NATIONAL PARK FEES & NONRESIDENT SURCHARGES

### CRITICAL: 2026 Nonresident Fee for Mom (Canadian Citizen)

Starting January 1, 2026, non-U.S. residents age 16+ pay a **$100 per-person surcharge** at 11 designated parks. **Four parks on this trip are on the list:**

| Park | Standard Entry (per vehicle) | Mom's $100 Surcharge |
|------|:---:|:---:|
| Grand Canyon | $35 | YES - $100 |
| Grand Teton | $35 | YES - $100 |
| Yellowstone | $35 | YES - $100 |
| Glacier | $35 | YES - $100 |
| Arches | $30 | No (not on the list) |
| Canyonlands | $30 | No (not on the list) |

### Best Strategy for Passes

**Option A: Two separate passes**
| Pass | Cost | Covers |
|------|-----:|--------|
| America the Beautiful (Colin, US resident) | $80 | Vehicle entry at all 6 parks for Colin |
| Nonresident Annual Pass (Mom, Canadian) | $250 | Vehicle entry + waives $100 surcharge at all 4 designated parks |
| **Total** | **$330** | |

**Option B: Colin's pass only + Mom pays surcharges individually**
| Item | Cost |
|------|-----:|
| America the Beautiful (Colin) | $80 |
| Mom's surcharge: Grand Canyon | $100 |
| Mom's surcharge: Grand Teton | $100 |
| Mom's surcharge: Yellowstone | $100 |
| Mom's surcharge: Glacier | $100 |

**RECOMMENDATION: Option A saves $150.** Buy the $250 Nonresident Annual Pass for Mom. It covers the surcharges at all 4 parks AND provides entry for up to 3 additional adults in her party (so it actually covers Colin too, making the $80 US resident pass optional). However, buying Colin's $80 pass separately is still good insurance in case they enter parks in separate vehicles or Mom's pass has any issues.

**Simplified approach:** Mom buys the $250 Nonresident Annual Pass. Colin buys the $80 US Resident pass. Total: $330. This gives redundant coverage and zero hassle at park gates.

## 11. MONEY-SAVING TIPS

### Flights (save $100-200)
- Book Alaska flights 45-60 days out (now is the optimal window for May 15)
- Set Google Flights price alerts for all routes
- Consider Southwest for SEA-PHX (free checked bags, flexible cancellation, not on Google Flights)
- Check if Mom has Aeroplan points for the MSO-YYZ leg

### Car Rental (save $100-300)
- Book via Costco Travel: free additional driver, no cancellation fee
- Monitor with AutoSlash.com: auto-rebooking if prices drop
- Use AARP discount at Avis/Budget for 30-35% off
- Decline rental counter insurance if credit card provides CDW (Chase Sapphire Preferred/Reserve, Amex Platinum all include primary CDW)

### Accommodation (save $200-400)
- Use Airbnb for multi-night stays (Moab 3 nights, Jackson 4 nights, Glacier 4 nights) for potential savings
- In Jackson, consider staying in Driggs, ID (30 min away) at $100/night vs $130 in Jackson = $120 savings over 4 nights
- Check for AAA/AARP hotel discounts at all properties
- Booking.com Genius discounts (10-20% off) if you have status

### Food (save $200-400)
- Cook 4-5 dinners in at Airbnb/condo kitchens (Glacier has full kitchen for 4 nights)
- Pack picnic lunches from grocery stores (saves $10-15/day over restaurants)
- Hit hotel free breakfasts hard (12 of 20 nights have this)
- Costco in Kalispell for Glacier-week groceries
- Bring reusable water bottles (fill at hotel/visitor centers instead of buying)

### Parks (save $150)
- Mom's $250 Nonresident Annual Pass saves $150 vs paying $100 surcharges at 4 individual parks
- Verify if Mom's pass covers the whole vehicle (it covers passholder + 3 additional adults)

### Other
- Bear spray: Buy Costco double-pack ($40) instead of 2 singles ($60-100)
- Or rent bear spray at Glacier Outfitters (~$10/day) if only using for 3-4 hiking days

## 12. TRAVEL INSURANCE RECOMMENDATION

### For Colin (US domestic traveler)
- **Trip cancellation insurance** is optional for domestic travel
- **Cost:** ~$80-150 for a 21-day domestic trip covering ~$4,000 in prepaid expenses
- **Worth it if:** Non-refundable flights/hotels represent significant money. Given most bookings have free cancellation, this is a "nice to have" but not critical.

### For Mom (Canadian citizen traveling in the US) -- STRONGLY RECOMMENDED

**This is not optional.** Ontario's OHIP covers roughly $400 CAD/day for out-of-country hospitalization. A single ER visit in the US can cost $5,000-50,000+. A hospital stay can exceed $10,000 USD per day.

| Coverage Type | Est. Cost (CAD) | Notes |
|---------------|----------------:|-------|
| Travel medical insurance (21 days, age 80) | $300-600 | Depends on pre-existing conditions, medical questionnaire |
| Comprehensive (medical + trip cancellation + baggage) | $400-800 | Recommended for full peace of mind |

**Key providers for Canadian seniors:**
- Manulife (up to $10M CAD medical coverage)
- Medipac (specializes in snowbirds/seniors, up to $5M USD)
- TuGo
- CAA Travel Insurance
- Allianz

**Important considerations for an 80-year-old traveler:**
- Most policies require a medical questionnaire at age 80+
- Pre-existing condition stability clause: conditions must be stable for 90-180 days prior to departure
- Some insurers (TD) limit trip duration to 4-16 days for 80+, so check carefully
- Buy within 14-21 days of booking to unlock pre-existing condition waivers
- Bring a copy of the policy, emergency contact card, and list of medications

**Budget for Mom's travel insurance:** $400-700 CAD (~$290-510 USD)
